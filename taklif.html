Tehran

Tehran is the capital city of Iran. It is one of the largest and most important cities in the country. Tehran is known for its museums, parks, universities and busy streets.

Isfahan

Isfahan is one of the most famous historical cities in Iran. It is known for its beautiful architecture, bridges, mosques and historical buildings.

Shiraz

Shiraz is a beautiful city in southern Iran. It is famous for its gardens, poetry, literature and historical places such as Persepolis.

Tabriz

Tabriz is a historic city in northwestern Iran. It is famous for its traditional bazaar, culture and historical buildings.
